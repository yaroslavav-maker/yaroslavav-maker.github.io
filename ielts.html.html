📈 Task 3: Describing Changes

📌 Instruction: Categorize each phrase as Increase ⬆️, Decrease ⬇️, or Stable ➡️
⬆️

INCREASE

  • increased / rose / grew / climbed
  • increased significantly
  • increased dramatically
  • doubled / tripled
  • by 10% (difference)
  • to 50% (final number)
⬇️

DECREASE

  • decreased / fell / dropped / declined
  • decreased slightly
  • decreased significantly
  • halved
  • by 15% (difference)
  • to 20% (final number)
➡️

STABLE

  • remained stable / constant / unchanged
  • stayed at / leveled off at
  • no significant change
  • remained at around

🎯 Band 9 Writing Structure

Paragraph 1: Introduction

Paraphrase the question (1 sentence)

Paragraph 2: Overview ⭐

2-3 main trends (NO specific data) - 25% of score!

Paragraph 3: Details 1

Largest segments + data + comparisons

Paragraph 4: Details 2

Smaller segments + changes over time
